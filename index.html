    Features

    Everything a vault needs.
    Nothing it doesn't.

    Lockly focuses on doing the essentials well — so your credentials stay organized, protected, and under your control.

    Password Manager

    Securely store and organize credentials. Logins, notes, and identities — all in one encrypted vault.

    Password Generator

    Generate strong, random passwords with adjustable length and character sets. No two accounts alike.

    Secure Notes

    Store sensitive text — Wi-Fi keys, recovery codes, documents — encrypted with everything else.

    Fast Search

    Find anything in your vault instantly. Search runs locally — your data is never sent anywhere.

    Auto Lock

    Automatically protect your vault when inactive. Walk away, and Lockly locks itself behind your master password.

    Cross-platform Planned

    Designed for desktop today, with mobile and browser support on the roadmap. Your vault follows you — on your terms.

    Security

    Security you can understand.

    No black boxes. Lockly's model is simple enough to explain — and open enough to verify.

    End-to-end encryption

    Your vault is encrypted on your device before it is stored or synced. Ciphertext is all anyone else ever sees.

    Zero-knowledge architecture

    Lockly has no access to your passwords — and no way to get one. Your master password never leaves your device.

    Local-first

    Your data remains on hardware you control. No mandatory cloud, no account servers, no third party in the middle.

    Open source

    The entire codebase can be inspected, audited, and forked. Trust is earned in the open — not claimed in a policy.

    How it works

    Three steps. Ten seconds.

    01

    Create your vault

    Choose a master password. Your vault is encrypted on your device before anything is stored.

    02

    Add your credentials

    Save logins, secure notes, and identities — organized, searchable, and always encrypted.

    03

    Unlock and use them securely

    Unlock with your master password when you need it. Lockly re-locks itself when you're done.
